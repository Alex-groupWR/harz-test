Описание

Designed for printing models that have higher requirements for mechanical properties. Durable, non-shrinking and odorless.

The fully cured polymer is very hard and elastic the same time. This allows you to print functional parts that can withstand mechanical stress.

Cured models are resistant to decay and can withstand high temperatures (up to 100 ° C), which makes it possible to use models to create silicone molds by cold and hot vulcanization. Works with two-component silicone on tin catalysts.

Характеристики

  • Colored transparent liquid Appearance
  • Clear, Natural Clear, Grey, Black, White, Color
  • Odorless Odor
  • 0.8-1 Pa*s Viscosity according to Brookfield
  • 45 N/mm2 Tensile strength
  • 4-6% Elongation at break
  • 87-92 Hardness Shore D
  • <0.5% Shrinkage

Видео о продукте

Преимущества

SAFE

Resin does not contain solvents and volatile monomers, irritating compounds. No fumes, odor and allergic reaction. Сan be used in lab or even at home

LOW SHRINKAGE

Shrinkage less than 0.5% gives you the same size like 3D model on your computer and it doesn’t change size after postcuring

COMPOSITION STABILITY

Pigments and fillers are well stabilized, no sedimentation and flocculation occur ang no foam appears

HIGH CHEMICAL RESISTANCE

Resin is stable to solvents, aggressive inorganic media like acids and alkalis

BIOCOMPATIBLE

Produced from biocompatible raw material used in photopolymer teeth fillings. Meets the standard ISO 10993

STABLE COLOR

Depending on postcuring condi-tion A1 or A2 color is achievable. It is stable for a long time even in aggressive media.

UNIQUE MECHANICAL PROPERTIES

Very high hardness, tensile strength, abrasion and scratch resistance due to ceramic fillers
